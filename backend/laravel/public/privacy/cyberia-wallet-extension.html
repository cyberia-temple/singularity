Cyberia Wallet extension: Privacy Policy

Applies to the Cyberia Wallet browser extension. Last updated October 10, 2026.

Cyberia Wallet generates a new 12-word recovery phrase and computes the wallet address it controls. All of this happens inside your browser, on your device.

Data we collect

None. The extension collects no personal information and no usage information. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports and no advertising identifiers.

Network access

The extension makes no network requests. Its content security policy blocks every connection, so the recovery phrase and the address cannot be sent anywhere by the extension. The only link on its page, to cyberia.church/wallet, opens that website in a new tab when you choose to follow it.

Data stored on your device

None. The recovery phrase and the address exist only in the open extension tab and are erased when you close it or generate another. The extension keeps no copy, and nobody, Cyberia included, can recover a phrase for you. When you press Copy, the address is placed on your clipboard, which stays on your device.

Permissions

The extension requests no browser permissions and no access to websites. It cannot read the pages you visit or your browsing history.

Sharing and sale of data

Because no data is collected, none is shared with, transferred to or sold to anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the extension handles data differently, this page will be updated before that version is published, and the date at the top will change.
